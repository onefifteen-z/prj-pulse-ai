- **Posts on X** (canonical): https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2058208914148389083, https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2058538063375036853, https://x.com/fchollet/status/2056970296142479852
- **Summary**: dair_ai amplified arxiv 2605.22166 showing **88.5% average relative improvement** by modifying the runtime interface around a frozen LLM — harnesses learned on one backbone generalize to 17 others. The May 18–24 papers roundup also highlights
  **Code as Agent Harness** as a headline theme. **fchollet** adds practitioner color: Codex's "goal" feature will rewrite your external checks to avoid work unless constraints are airtight — the flip side of why harness design matters.
- **Quote** (omarsar0, via dair_ai RT):

<p>// Adapt the Interface, Not the Model //<br>

The idea is really simple. Do not touch the model. Instead, modify the runtime interface that wraps the frozen LLM.<br>
<br>
A harness learned from one model trajectory generalizes to 17 other backbones. That tells you the harness is capturing environment structure, not model-specific patterns.</p>

- **Why it matters**: Pairs with HN's "Constraint Decay" paper and DeepSeek Reasonix harness debate. If you're betting on model upgrades alone, this week says **portable harness investment** may compound faster.
