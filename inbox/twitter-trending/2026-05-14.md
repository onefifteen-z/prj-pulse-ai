## 🧪 Emerging Signals

### 🔥 Frozen‑model attention/memory hacks: get more from what you already trained

- **Mentioned by**: rasbt, dair_ai (amplifier)
- **Posts on X**: https://x.com/rasbt/status/2054543968344412621, https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2054600147020222630
- **Summary**: Sebastian Raschka highlights **Lighthouse Attention** (Nous Research) — a subquadratic wrapper used during most of pretraining and then *removed* before deployment, recovering vanilla full‑attention quality at much cheaper long‑context training. dair_ai amplifies **δ‑mem**, a fixed‑size online associative‑memory state that augments a *frozen* full‑attention backbone with low‑rank attention corrections (no fine‑tuning, no backbone swap, no context extension) — an 8×8 state lifts average score by 1.10×, with 1.31× on MemoryAgentBench and 1.20× on LoCoMo.
- **Quote** (Raschka on why this matters operationally):

<p>Interesting paper. What I like about this is that it is a relatively low-commitment attention modification.<br>
<br>
I.e., one can use it during most of training, switch back to vanilla attention near the end, and recover roughly the same modeling performance as if full attention had been used the whole time.</p>

- **Why it matters**: Both results push the "do more with the model you already have" frontier — train cheaper, deploy frozen, bolt memory on. For infra/research teams the cost story is shifting from "scale context" to "augment with side state and removable wrappers."
